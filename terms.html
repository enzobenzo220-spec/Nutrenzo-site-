Effective date: October 23, 2026

Text START to (440) 616-7008 to receive text updates from Nutrenzo about your gym inquiries, free trial bookings, and appointment confirmations.

1. Acceptance of terms

By calling or texting the business phone number associated with this program, by checking the text-message consent box on nutrenzo.com, or by otherwise opting in, you agree to these Terms and Conditions for the Nutrenzo SMS messaging program. Nutrenzo is operated by Vincenzio James in Cleveland, Ohio.

2. Description of service

Nutrenzo sends automated text message replies on behalf of local gyms and fitness studios when a call to their business line is missed, and supports two-way text conversation to answer basic questions and schedule free trial visits or appointments. We also text people who request information from Nutrenzo directly.

3. How you opt in

  • Check the text-message consent box on a form at nutrenzo.com.
  • Text START, YES or UNSTOP to (440) 616-7008.
  • Agree verbally when you call and are asked whether we may text you back at that number.

Consent is not a condition of purchase.

4. Message types, frequency and costs

  • You may receive messages about your inquiry, including replies to your questions, trial or appointment confirmations, reminders and follow-ups.
  • Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Charges from your wireless carrier are your responsibility.

5. Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to cancel further messages. Reply HELP for assistance. After you text STOP, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.
