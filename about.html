My story

Hi, I’m Enzo James.

I’m a NASM-certified personal trainer and nutrition coach with a degree in Nutrition and minors in Exercise Science and Management. I work on a gym floor, and I keep seeing the same thing: someone DMs at night asking about prices, calls during a busy class, or fills out the website form. By the time anyone has a free minute, they’ve moved on.

Owner-run gyms don’t lose those people because their coaching isn’t good. They lose them because nobody can watch the phone and inbox all day while running the gym.

So I built Nutrenzo: an AI that answers every call, DM and form in under 60 seconds, books the trial and follows up, the way your best front-desk person would if you had one.
