The problem

You’re paying for leads you never answer.

Most independent gyms don’t have someone watching the phone and inbox all day. You’re coaching, cleaning or finally home. By the time you reply, the lead has booked somewhere else.

  • Missed calls with no text-backThey call during a class, get voicemail and call the next gym on Google.
  • DMs answered the next day“How much is it?” at 10 PM is a buying question. A reply 12 hours later usually isn’t.
  • Trials booked by handBack-and-forth texts to find a time, then no reminder, then a no-show.

In a Harvard Business Review study of over a million sales leads, companies that tried to reach a lead within an hour were nearly 7 times as likely to have a real conversation with them as companies that waited even one hour longer.
